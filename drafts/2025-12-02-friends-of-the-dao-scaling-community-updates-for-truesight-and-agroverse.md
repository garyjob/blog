- **Burying Asks**: Tucking a desperate ?We need intros to X? at the bottom after 800 words. Nobody gets there.

#### What ?Right? Looks Like

- **Clear Asks**: 1-3 specific requests, labeled as such. ?Ask 1: Looking for beta testers in fintech. Know anyone?? Make it easy to say yes.
