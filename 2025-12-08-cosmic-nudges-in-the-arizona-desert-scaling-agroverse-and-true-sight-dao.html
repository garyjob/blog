Cosmic Nudges in the Arizona Desert: Scaling Agroverse and True Sight DAO

December 7, 2025 Technology

I’m standing at the sink, hands in soapy water, scrubbing dishes after finishing up a sales volume chart for Agroverse.shop. My mind’s wandering, staring at a random spot in the northern night sky, while I crunch numbers in my head. How many mega yearly iterations of our supply chain do we need to keep quadrupling sales volume every year to hit that big, audacious goal of USD$100 million in annual revenue? I’m deep in this mental gymnastics when I hit iteration 7—projecting roughly $150 million for that year—and right at that exact moment, a shooting star slices across the very patch of sky I’m fixated on. Talk about a cosmic wink.

Let me break this down. If each bag we sell at Agroverse.shop goes for $17—and each sale plants a tree—by year 7, we’re looking at a cumulative 11,565,141 trees in the ground. That’s about 11,565 hectares of restored land, smashing past our target of 10,000 hectares. I couldn’t help but smirk to myself. Is this the universe’s way of saying, “Yeah, Gary, this is your North Star”? I’ve been telling folks this adventure with Agroverse and True Sight DAO is the kind that could span a lifetime. But if this geometric growth model actually plays out, we might hit the target in just 7 years. How wild is that?

Title: Chasing a Cosmic North Star with Agroverse and True Sight DAO

#### When the Universe Signals Your Path
That shooting star moment got me reflecting on what we’re building here. Agroverse.shop isn’t just about selling eco-friendly products—it’s about regenerating the planet, one tree at a time. And True Sight DAO, the decentralized backbone behind it, is how we’re ensuring transparency and community governance as we scale. But scaling to this level—quadrupling growth every year for 7 iterations—requires more than just ambition. It’s a massive, all-encompassing system design experiment that touches every part of what we do, from supply chains to community engagement.

#### The 4X Challenge: Reinventing at Every Step
Here’s the thing about aiming for 4X growth with each iteration: it forces you to rethink everything. At every stage, we have to critically examine our entire setup—logistics, partnerships, tech infrastructure, even how we communicate our mission—and ask, “What needs to change to make the next leap possible?” My gut tells me that if we do reach iteration 7, the way our systems look now versus how they’ll look then will be night and day. We’re not just scaling a business; we’re evolving an ecosystem.

  • Supply Chain: How do we source sustainably at 4X the volume without compromising quality or ethics?
  • Community: How do we keep True Sight DAO’s decentralized ethos intact as millions more join the mission?
  • Impact: How do we measure and verify the environmental impact of 11 million trees while staying transparent?

#### Reflections for the Evening
This whole experiment feels like a thrilling ride. It’s not just about hitting revenue targets or planting trees—it’s about proving that a decentralized, mission-driven model can scale massively while staying true to its roots. I’m curious to see how this unfolds over the next few years, especially as we iterate and adapt. Will we look back at iteration 7 and laugh at how naive we were at iteration 1? Probably. But that’s the beauty of it—growth isn’t just about numbers; it’s about transformation.

What about you—how do you approach massive, multi-year goals? Got any “shooting star” moments that made you feel like you’re on the right path? I’d love to hear your stories while I sip my cup of cacao and dream about iteration 7.
